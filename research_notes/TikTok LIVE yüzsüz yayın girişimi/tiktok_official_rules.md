# TikTok LIVE official rules for a faceless, voice-free, partly automated, AI-driven, gift-triggered interactive stream (Turkey focus, as of 2026-11-21)

Method note: In this session, direct page fetches of every tiktok.com host (www.tiktok.com, support.tiktok.com, t.tiktok.com) and most third-party sites were blocked by the network sandbox. Every "verbatim" quote below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited page, not from a full read of it. The wording matches across several searches, but the report writer should treat it as close to verbatim, not certified. Official pages are labelled **[OFFICIAL]** and everything else **[SECONDARY]**. Where a fact may vary by region or could not be confirmed for Turkey, the notes say so.

---

## 1. LIVE eligibility: age, followers, regions, Turkey, Gifts/Diamonds availability, agencies

### Takeaway
Officially, going LIVE and sending or receiving Gifts both require age 18+ (19+ for Gifts in South Korea). The commonly cited follower threshold is 1,000, and TikTok says it "may vary by region". No official page found states a Turkey-specific threshold or a Turkey exclusion. Secondary evidence strongly suggests that LIVE Gifts and Diamond withdrawals work in Turkey, and that TikTok runs a LIVE agency (Creator Network) ecosystem there.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] Community Guidelines, "Accounts and Features":** users "must be 18 and older to go LIVE and to send gifts to a creator during a LIVE session". LIVE gifting is listed among monetization features that require 18+. LIVE sessions are also restricted to 18+ audiences when they contain content "not suitable for young people". — [TikTok Accounts and Features](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/accounts-features)
- **[OFFICIAL] Virtual Items Policy:** "Users can only buy Coins and Gifts, send Gifts to others, receive Gifts with monetary value, earn Diamonds and withdraw Diamonds if they are aged 18 (or age of majority in your jurisdiction) or older." — [Virtual Items Policy (global)](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/virtual-items-policy/en). Separate versions exist for the rest of the world ("row"), the EEA and the US: [ROW](https://t.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/virtual-items/en), [EEA](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/eea/virtual-items/en), [US](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/us/virtual-items/en)
- **[SECONDARY, citing the Help Center]** "To send or receive LIVE Gifts, you must be at least 18, or 19 in South Korea." LIVE Gifts also require "a supported location and an account in good standing". — [Thundrr](https://www.thundrr.co/blog/tiktok-live-requirements/); [TikTokStats](https://tiktokstats.com/guides/tiktok-live-content-monetization-requirements-2026-guide)
- **[SECONDARY, citing the Help Center]** "TikTok's Help Center lists 1,000 followers to go LIVE and notes that this may vary across regions." TikTok raised the minimum age to host LIVE from 16 to 18 in late 2022. — [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/followers-needed-for-tiktok-live/); [Thundrr](https://www.thundrr.co/blog/tiktok-live-requirements/). Some blogs report creators going LIVE with fewer than 1,000 followers, but only anecdotally. One "10,000 followers" figure refers to a different program.
- **[SECONDARY]** If TikTok cannot confirm the user's age, the user cannot go LIVE. The in-app "Get access to go LIVE" requirements tab is the authoritative check for an individual account. — [StreamHouse](https://streamhouse.co/blog/tiktok-live-requirements-access); [HowSociable](https://howsociable.com/blog/how-many-followers-to-go-live-on-tiktok)
- **[SECONDARY, Turkey]** Turkish creator guides describe Turkish streamers receiving Gifts, converting them to "elmas" (Diamonds) and withdrawing via PayPal or bank IBAN. One guide says the Creator Rewards Program and TikTok Shop seller centre are not available in Turkey, while LIVE gifts are open to Turkish creators. — [Biroku](https://www.biroku.com.tr/tiktokta-canli-yayin-hediyeleri-nakde-nasil-cevrilir-adim-adim/); [Hyper Media](https://hypermedia.com.tr/blog/tiktok-canli-yayin-parasi-nasil-cekilir-odeme-rehberi-2026); [Dijital Ajanslar](https://www.dijitalajanslar.com/tiktok-para-kazanma/)
- **[SECONDARY, Turkish news, Nov 2026]** A November 2026 prosecution (see Q9) describes Turkish streamers who "converted these gifts to cash" ("Fenomenler bu hediyeleri nakde çevirdi") after TikTok's commission. This is indirect evidence that Gift-to-cash withdrawal works in Turkey. — [Nefes](https://www.nefes.com.tr/tiktok-fenomenlerine-sanal-hediye-gondererek-para-akladilar-8-ilde-operasyon-163636); [Memurlar.net](https://www.memurlar.net/haber/1183759/tiktok-ta-1-9-milyarlik-kara-para-trafigi.html)
- **[OFFICIAL-adjacent: TikTok job postings, Turkey]** TikTok is hiring in Istanbul for "TikTok LIVE Agency Operations Manager – Turkey", "Agency Partnerships Manager, TikTok LIVE – Turkey", "TikTok LIVE – Partner Growth & Incubation Manager (Turkey)" and "TikTok LIVE Community Agency Manager – Turkey". The postings describe recruiting and onboarding "agencies, MCNs, and talent organizations to strengthen the TikTok LIVE ecosystem in Turkey", plus agency education programs and monetization growth campaigns. — [The Muse: Partner Growth & Incubation](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/tiktok/tiktok-live-partner-growth-incubation-manager-turkey); [The Muse: Agency Partnerships](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/tiktok/agency-partnerships-manager-tiktok-live-turkey); [The Muse: Agency Ops](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/tiktok/tiktok-live-agency-operations-manager-turkey-00d1d7)
- **[SECONDARY, Turkish news]** Habertürk reports that agencies in Turkey's TikTok LIVE economy earn on performance, not per streamer, and that LIVE-driven income was expected to approach roughly a quarter of Turkey's content-creator economy in 2025. It names Play Studio as an example agency. — [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/tiktok-live-ekonomisinde-yeni-is-modeli-ajanslar-yayincidan-degil-performanstan-kazaniyor-3866247-teknoloji)
- **[SECONDARY]** Turkish blogs mention agency-linked "diamond target bonuses", for example 50,000 Diamonds a month. Not verified with TikTok. — [Juntire](https://juntire.com/blog/tiktok-para-kazanma-2026-rehber)

### Inferences
- The global and ROW Virtual Items Policy with its 18+ rule almost certainly governs Turkey. TikTok publishes separate EEA and US versions, and Turkey is in neither. The policy says "18 (or age of majority in your jurisdiction)". Turkey's age of majority is generally 18 under the Turkish Civil Code, but this research did not source that, so verify it.
- TikTok's own LIVE agency hiring in Istanbul makes it very likely that LIVE Gifts and Diamonds are fully live in Turkey and that agency (Backstage / LIVE Creator Network) affiliation is a practical route for Turkish creators. Agencies are often how creators get stream keys, LIVE Studio access and support (see Q2).

### Gaps
- No official TikTok page was found that lists the countries where LIVE Gifts are supported or that names Turkey. Availability in Turkey rests on strong secondary and indirect evidence.
- The exact follower threshold for Turkish accounts could not be confirmed, because the Help Center page was unreachable. TikTok says it varies by region.
- No official TikTok page in English or Turkish was found describing the "LIVE Creator Network" / Backstage agency program for Turkey.

## 2. Streaming from a PC: TikTok LIVE Studio vs RTMP stream key / OBS

### Takeaway
TikTok LIVE Studio is the official Windows desktop app (64-bit Windows 10+), and access is gated by account eligibility. A stream key or RTMP URL for OBS-type software is granted only to some accounts. Meeting a follower threshold does not unlock it automatically, and agencies are a commonly reported route. LIVE Studio includes an official "Interactive Games" tool where Gifts trigger in-game effects. Sources conflict on the details.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] LIVE Studio download page:** supports only 64-bit Windows 10 and newer. It lists minimum, "General LIVE" and "Gaming LIVE" hardware tiers, the minimum being an Intel Core i3 9th Gen or AMD Ryzen 5 3000, a GTX 10-series or AMD Radeon GPU, and 8 GB RAM. — [Download LIVE Studio](https://www.tiktok.com/studio/download?lang=en)
- **[OFFICIAL, older]** The LIVE Studio Operation Manual help article is about 1,000 days old and still refers to Windows 8.1, so it is likely outdated. — [LIVE Studio Operation Manual](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/1023/tiktok-live-studio-operation-manual_en-US?lang=en)
- **[SECONDARY]** Access to LIVE Studio is gated, and the download button appears only for approved accounts. One summary says TikTok's LIVE Studio FAQ sets 1,000 followers for gaming creators and 10,000 for everyone else. Other guides cite an account at least 30 days old, recent LIVE activity and good standing, and note that access is not guaranteed. — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/tiktok-live-studio/); [SMMNut](https://smmnut.com/blog/tiktok-live-studio-guide-2026/); [TokConnect](https://tokconnect.com/guides/tiktok-live-studio/)
- **[SECONDARY]** Stream key / RTMP: TikTok's public help pages "don't list a follower count that guarantees a stream key", and a key does not follow automatically from meeting LIVE eligibility. Only accounts with RTMP access see a server URL and key, located in TikTok Studio's LIVE tab or the LIVE Center producer page depending on the guide. "Some creators get LIVE Studio access but never see an RTMP key." LIVE Studio itself needs no key. — [Upstream](https://upstream.so/blog/tiktok-stream-key/); [ScrollPlay](https://scrollplay.live/guides/tiktok-live-studio-vs-obs); [Streamer Toolkit](https://streamertoolkit.com/guides/how-to-stream-on-tiktok-live-with-obs/)
- **[SECONDARY, conflicting]** Hollyland says TikTok generates a new stream key each time a LIVE session is started. Others describe a persistent key. A secondary guide says LIVE Studio for macOS was in beta as of April 2026. — [Hollyland](https://www.hollyland.com/blog/topics/get-your-tiktok-stream-key); [ScrollPlay](https://scrollplay.live/guides/tiktok-live-studio-vs-obs)
- **[SECONDARY]** A key-free OBS route uses OBS Virtual Camera as a source in LIVE Studio or LIVE Manager. One such workflow (LIVE Manager) is limited to eligible US TikTok Shop accounts. — [Upstream](https://upstream.so/blog/tiktok-stream-key/)
- **[SECONDARY, Aug 2024]** A forum post reports that LIVE Studio "requires a camera source to be active at all times to be able to live stream". The workaround feeds an OBS virtual camera into LIVE Studio carrying a green-screen image or a VTuber avatar. Other guides say no camera is needed. Treat both as unverified. — [KatsBits forum](https://www.katsbits.com/community/index.php/topic,1319.0.html); [TokTutorials VTuber guide](https://www.toktutorials.com/post/how-to-be-a-vtuber-on-tiktok-live)
- **[OFFICIAL feature, described via third-party walkthroughs]** LIVE Studio has an "Interactive Games" library under Tools. Its help text reads: "Interactive Games lets viewers influence the game you are playing by sending Gifts that trigger in-game effects." One reviewer's setup showed 16 TikTok-provided games and a "Viewer Wishes" panel that tells viewers which Gift does what. — [TokTutorials](https://www.toktutorials.com/post/how-to-set-up-interactive-games-in-tiktok-live-studio); [PlayLiveTikTok setup guide](https://www.playlivetiktok.com/setup-guide)
- **[OFFICIAL] LIVE Studio help, game-specific best practice:** TikTok publishes game guides such as "How to go LIVE with Grand Theft Auto VI on TikTok LIVE Studio", which implies that game capture is a supported use. — [LIVE Studio Help: GTA VI](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Best-practice/How-to-go-LIVE-with-GTA-VI-on-TikTok-LIVE-Studio)
- **[SECONDARY]** Background: Wikipedia reports that the 2021 invitation-only LIVE Studio release appeared to be based on OBS Studio, in breach of OBS's license. — [Wikipedia: OBS Studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OBS_Studio)

### Inferences
- For a PC-rendered AI game, LIVE Studio with Window or Game Capture plus its native Interactive Games and Gift-trigger tooling is the official, lowest-risk route. Using OBS through an RTMP key depends on TikTok granting that key, often via an agency.
- Third-party gift-to-action tools such as TikFinity or BLIVE read the LIVE event stream. TikTok's own Interactive Games show that the gift-to-effect concept is sanctioned, but the September 2025 Community Guidelines make creators responsible for anything third-party tools put on screen (see Q3).

### Gaps
- The official LIVE Studio eligibility FAQ and stream-key rules could not be read directly. Thresholds conflict across sources (1,000 vs 10,000 followers).
- Whether LIVE Studio currently requires an active camera source is unconfirmed.
- No official information was found on whether Turkish accounts get RTMP keys on the same terms.

## 3. LIVE content standards: unoriginal, pre-recorded, looped, static, unattended, automated or AI-operated streams, creator presence, AI labels, avatars

### Takeaway
This is the biggest risk for the concept. TikTok's official LIVE Monetization Guidelines make content ineligible for monetization when it is "generated or operated primarily through automated systems, such as AI or other third-party tools, without meaningful creator presence, interaction, or facilitation of the LIVE experience". Content that is "largely passive, repetitive, unattended, or lacking meaningful viewer engagement" is also ineligible. LIVE Studio guidance warns that a no-face setup can be read as low interaction or a recording. A faceless stream is not banned outright, but a stream with no human present is very likely to be demonetized or excluded from recommendation.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] LIVE Monetization Guidelines (LIVE Creator Hub)** frame monetization as support for authentic LIVE experiences that reflect genuine creator contribution. Ineligible content includes:
  - content "generated or operated primarily through automated systems, such as AI or other third-party tools, without meaningful creator presence, interaction, or facilitation of the LIVE experience";
  - "Content that is largely passive, repetitive, unattended, or lacking meaningful viewer engagement may be ineligible for monetization." The example given is "sleeping during LIVE or performing routine or task-based activities without interaction or engagement with viewers";
  - pre-recorded, spliced or looping videos presented as if live, and prolonged blurred or static screens;
  - "If a guest shares ineligible content, the creator's LIVE may be subject to enforcement actions, including restrictions on LIVE monetization features."
  — [LIVE Monetization Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/rules_and_guidance/live_monetization_guidelines)
- **[OFFICIAL] LIVE Creator Hub, feature guidelines:** eligibility is lost for fake engagement used to ask for Gifts, including through "mechanical/overly-repetitive activities or automatic systems/programmed loops". The wording is close to verbatim from a snippet. — [LIVE Creator Hub feature guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/live-feature-guidelines)
- **[OFFICIAL] LIVE Studio Help, "Community Guidelines for LIVE creators":**
  - "Using a no-face effect might be easily regarded as low interaction, and LIVE might be regarded as a recording."
  - "These problems can be identified by the lack of interaction and still images, making it more likely to be recognized as recorded content during LIVE."
  - It advises against streaming previously recorded material, recommends staying on screen for the whole broadcast, says to pause the LIVE rather than leave it running when stepping away, and warns that video, screen capture and TV casting carry copyright risk.
  — [LIVE Studio Help: Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Before-you-go-LIVE/Community-Guidelines?lang=en)
- **[OFFICIAL] LIVE Studio Help, GTA VI guide:** "Long cutscenes without your face on screen or any commentary can make your LIVE appear pre-recorded." — [LIVE Studio Help: GTA VI](https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Best-practice/How-to-go-LIVE-with-GTA-VI-on-TikTok-LIVE-Studio)
- **[OFFICIAL] Community Guidelines, effective 13 Sept 2025, LIVE section:** "LIVE creators are responsible for anything that happens during their sessions—even when it involves third-party tools like voice-to-text software, real-time translation, or on-screen comment displays. Creators are expected to actively monitor and manage any tools they use…" Repeated issues with third-party tools "may lead to restrictions on using LIVE or LIVE features". The LIVE section also refers to a For You feed (FYF) standards threshold, with temporary restrictions for creators who repeatedly fall short of it. — [Accounts and Features, 2025H2 version](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/accounts-features?cgversion=2025H2update); news coverage: [TechCrunch, 15 Aug 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/15/tiktoks-new-guidelines-add-subtle-changes-for-live-creators-ai-content-and-more/); [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/news/tiktok-updates-community-guidelines-with-new-rules-for-live-creators-and-ai-content/)
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok Shop context]** TikTok Shop seller rules prohibit pre-recorded content in livestreams, including switching between live and playback, and require livestreams to be real-time. — [Seller University PH](https://seller-ph.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654197957084929&lang=en); [Seller University UK](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7841352058554114)
- **[SECONDARY]** Per a third-party summary, LIVE sessions that stream unoriginal content without new edits are ineligible for the For You feed. Another notes that TikTok does not publish a complete list of recommendation-restriction triggers. — [AwareXOne](https://www.awarexone.com/research/tiktok-live-restricted-unoriginal-content); [Sirency](https://www.sirency.com/blog/tiktok-live-not-eligible-for-recommendation)
- **[OFFICIAL] AI-generated content (Integrity & Authenticity):** "creators must label AI-generated or significantly edited content that shows realistic-looking scenes or people. Unlabeled content may be removed, restricted, or labeled by our team." — [Community Guidelines: Integrity and Authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)
- **[OFFICIAL] Support, AI-generated content:** TikTok encourages labels on fully generated or significantly edited content and may remove unlabeled AI content that misleads. Content made only with TikTok effects is labelled automatically. — [Support: How is AI-generated content labeled](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content)
- **[SECONDARY]** TikTok may auto-apply an AI label when C2PA or Content Credentials provenance data is present, and the label may not be removable. Some 2026 guides claim stricter auto-detection that could not be verified. — [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/tiktoks-2026-ai-labeling-rules-and-what-they-signal-for-platform-governance/); [AuditSocials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/tiktok-ai-content-disclosure-rules-2026)
- **[SECONDARY]** VTuber and avatar setups are widely described as workable on TikTok LIVE, typically VTube Studio feeding a virtual camera into LIVE Studio. No TikTok rule banning avatars was found. — [TokTutorials VTuber](https://www.toktutorials.com/post/how-to-be-a-vtuber-on-tiktok-live)

### Inferences
- **Faceless or avatar on its own:** not prohibited by any official text found. Official LIVE Studio guidance treats "no-face" plus low interaction as a signal that the LIVE is a recording, which risks reduced distribution and monetization.
- **Voice-free:** no rule requires a voice. TikTok's examples, though, use "commentary" and "interaction" as the signs of a live stream. A voice-free stream therefore needs other real-time human signals, such as a human replying to comments in real time with on-screen text, live moderation, and visible responses to specific viewers.
- **Partly automated or AI-driven:** the operative test is "primarily through automated systems … without meaningful creator presence, interaction, or facilitation". A human operator who is present, moderates, talks to viewers through text and steers the game reduces the risk. A 24/7 unattended loop is squarely within the ineligible category, and with no human it is also a "programmed loop" in the gift-baiting sense.
- **AI visuals:** a stylised, non-realistic game or avatar probably does not trigger the mandatory label, which applies to "realistic-looking scenes or people". A realistic AI human avatar or voice would need labelling and carries impersonation risk.
- The creator is responsible for third-party tools, so any AI-generated on-screen text, TTS of viewer comments or game output that breaks the Community Guidelines counts against the account.

### Gaps
- No official LIVE-specific rule on AI avatars or VTubers, or on labelling synthetic avatars during LIVE, was found.
- No official definition was found of "meaningful creator presence" (for example, whether it can be shown without face or voice).
- The full official list of FYF-ineligible LIVE content and the "FYF standards threshold" details could not be read directly.

## 4. Gift-related rules: solicitation and gift baiting, gifts in exchange for outcomes, gambling and chance mechanics, and whether gift-triggered games are allowed

### Takeaway
Gift-triggered game mechanics are an officially supported product category: LIVE Studio Interactive Games, Interactive Gifts and the Effect House LIVE Gift tooling. The risk is not "gift → action" itself. It lies in (a) gift baiting and manipulation, including mechanical or programmed solicitation loops, and (b) anything resembling gambling, meaning paid chances to win something of value. TikTok bans gambling promotions and, in TikTok Shop, bans "lucky spins, wheels, draws" for giveaways. The September 2026 Community Guidelines reportedly made gambling restrictions global.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] LIVE Monetization Guidelines, "Fake engagement and Gift baiting":** "Content, behavior, or activities that seek to manipulate viewer engagement or monetization features are ineligible for monetization." The example list was truncated in the extracts. — [LIVE Monetization Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/rules_and_guidance/live_monetization_guidelines)
- **[OFFICIAL] LIVE Creator Hub:** fake engagement to ask for Gifts, including through "mechanical/overly-repetitive activities or automatic systems/programmed loops", affects eligibility. — [LIVE Creator Hub feature guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/live/creators/en-US/live-feature-guidelines)
- **[SECONDARY, paraphrasing the official list]** Gift-baiting examples include tricking viewers, asking mechanically and repeatedly, follow-for-follow trades, emotional pressure, asking for money for personal financial needs, and conditional exchanges ("I'll do X if you send me a gift"). Enforcement escalates from an in-app warning to temporary or permanent blocks on LIVE Gifts, LIVE Match and ranking features. — [TikFinity blog](https://blog.tikfinity.com/tiktok-live-rules/); [Streamer.guide](https://streamer.guide/guides/tiktok-live-rules); [Manychat](https://manychat.com/blog/tiktok-live-monetization/)
- **[SECONDARY, unverified]** One guide claims creators "are no longer allowed to actively ask for gifts during a live session". This could not be confirmed against TikTok text. — [TikTokStats (search snippet)](https://tiktokstats.com/guides/tiktok-live-content-monetization-requirements-2026-guide)
- **[OFFICIAL] Gift-triggered interactivity is sanctioned:** LIVE Studio "Interactive Games lets viewers influence the game you are playing by sending Gifts that trigger in-game effects." — [TokTutorials walkthrough](https://www.toktutorials.com/post/how-to-set-up-interactive-games-in-tiktok-live-studio). Effect House: "Interactive Gifts are virtual items that viewers can send to creators' content during their LIVEs." The LIVE Interactive Gift Effect Challenge "has come to an end". Giftopia is "TikTok LIVE's open-source artist program created to recognize and reward independent designers of animated and interactive TikTok LIVE Gifts". — [Effect House: LIVE Interactive Gift Effect Challenge](https://effecthouse.tiktok.com/latest/live-interactive-gift-effect-challenge); [Effect House: LIVE Gift](https://effecthouse.tiktok.com/live-gift?enter_method=top_navigation)
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok Shop, restated Nov 2026]** "Gambling promotions, including lotteries, sweepstakes, raffles, wagers, and paid games of chance, are prohibited on TikTok Shop." "For free giveaways, sellers and creators must only use the LIVE Giveaway feature." Seller guidance also bans using "lucky spins, wheels, draws, or other games for giveaways" to select winners or choose gifts or products. — [Covers.com, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.covers.com/industry/tiktok-reaffirms-ban-on-gambling-sweepstakes-promotions-nov-6-2026); [Deadspin](https://deadspin.com/legal-betting/tiktok-shop-restates-ban-on-gambling-sweepstakes-content/); [Seller University US: Gambling Policy](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2903157654996737&lang=en); [Seller University TH: Gambling, Gifting and Surprise-based Products](https://seller-th.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2624678623512321&lang=en)
- **[SECONDARY]** On LIVE, promoting betting sites, showing slot play, running raffles or hosting games of chance are described as prohibited, including "'gift to spin the wheel' formats when they cross into wagering". Another guide: "do not run raffles, lotteries, spin-the-wheel or random draws where a gift buys a chance at money, gift cards, items or other money-like prizes." Both are guide interpretations, not TikTok quotes. A guide citing LIVE Studio help says gift games must follow the Community Guidelines, "no gambling and no fake fundraising". — [Streamer.guide](https://streamer.guide/guides/tiktok-live-rules); [TikLiveGames](https://tiklivegames.io/articles/tiktok-live-gift-game-rules)
- **[SECONDARY] Community Guidelines effective 24 Sept 2026:** gambling (and firearms) content that was previously restricted only regionally now applies everywhere. One tracker reports three substantive changes to the regulated-goods policy. — [NoobClaw](https://noobclaw.com/blog/tiktok-community-guidelines-update-september-2026/); [Kabo](https://kabo.sh/guides/tiktok-community-guidelines-september-2026); [SocialScaleHub](https://www.socialscalehub.com/academy/tiktok-community-guidelines-september-24-2026)

### Inferences
- **Lower risk:** a deterministic gift → game action design, for example a gift spawns a unit, changes the weather or adds a power-up, where the viewer gets no prize or value and no random payout. This matches TikTok's own Interactive Games model.
- **High risk:** any mechanic where a gift buys a chance at a reward with real or transferable value. Examples are random prize wheels, lotteries, loot-box-style rewards, "top gifter wins X", and cash or gift-card prizes. This collides with TikTok's gambling rules and Turkish lottery and gambling law (see Q9).
- **Moderate risk:** an on-screen "send Gift X to make Y happen" menu is common and mirrors TikTok's own "Viewer Wishes" panel. Automated, repetitive TTS or overlay prompts asking for Gifts, especially with no human present, map onto "mechanical/overly-repetitive activities or automatic systems/programmed loops" and gift baiting.
- **Unclear:** "gift-for-outcome" promises ("if you send X, I'll do Y") are cited by secondary sources as gift baiting. TikTok's own Interactive Games are explicitly gift-for-in-game-effect, so the line seems to fall between in-game effects (sanctioned) and personal promises or pressure (baiting). That reading is inferred, not quoted.

### Gaps
- The full official "Gift baiting" example list could not be read verbatim (the extract was truncated).
- The verbatim official Community Guidelines "Regulated Goods & Commercial Activities – Gambling" text for the 24 Sept 2026 version could not be retrieved.
- No official statement was found that explicitly addresses third-party gift-triggered game overlays (TikFinity-style) as allowed or disallowed.

## 5. Minors: LIVE audiences, under-18 gifting, and Turkey

### Takeaway
Officially, users under 18 cannot host LIVE, buy Coins, or send or receive Gifts, and LIVEs with content "not suitable for young people" are restricted to adult viewers. Turkey's new Law 7578 bars under-15s from social networks from 1 Nov 2026 and requires a separate service for 15–17-year-olds. Turkish authorities have prosecuted cases of minors used in LIVE for gifts.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** "Users must be 18 and older to go LIVE and to send gifts to a creator during a LIVE session." LIVE sessions are restricted to 18+ when they contain content or behaviour unsuitable for young people. — [Accounts and Features](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/accounts-features)

- A gift-driven game will attract teenage viewers. ## 6. Diamond payout mechanics: conversion, rate setting, minimum withdrawal, methods, creator share

### Takeaway
Officially, TikTok sets the Gift → Diamond → cash rate "in its absolute and sole discretion", shows the withdrawal rate only at the point of withdrawal, and does not guarantee timing. TikTok publishes no fixed percentage. Its current official framing, "Scaled LIVE rewards", lets creators earn up to 53% in LIVE rewards (40% Per-LIVE Missions + 13% Weekly Missions), tied to performance and compliance. The widely quoted "~50% / $0.005 per Diamond" and "$50 or $100 minimum" figures are unofficial.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL] Virtual Items Policy:**
  - The Diamond rate is "determined by TikTok from time to time in its absolute and sole discretion."
  - "The rate of withdrawal will be displayed at the point of withdrawal."
  - "TikTok does not guarantee fulfilment within a specific period of time."
  - Withdrawal rates and daily limits are shown at withdrawal, not fixed in the policy.
  — [Virtual Items Policy (global)](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/virtual-items-policy/en); [ROW](https://t.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/virtual-items/en)

  - Creators can collect "up to 53%" of LIVE rewards: 40% from Per-LIVE Missions and 13% from Weekly Missions.
  - "Most creators are now eligible to receive up to 53% in rewards weekly if they hit the highest tiers."
  - Per-LIVE missions depend on LIVE duration, new unique followers and adherence to the LIVE monetization and community guidelines. Weekly missions depend on LIVE frequency, engagement and Creator League performance.

## 9. Turkey-specific legal context: Law 7253 (social media), gambling and lottery law, AML, age rules

### Takeaway
TikTok complies with Turkey's 2020 social media law (Law 7253, amending Law 5651): it has a local representative and faces fines, ad bans and up to 90% bandwidth throttling if it does not comply. Turkish law reserves cash games of chance to Milli Piyango İdaresi and requires permission for non-cash lotteries and draws, and illegal betting is prosecuted under Law 7258. In Nov 2026, prosecutors pursued a 1.9 billion TRY case of laundering illegal-betting proceeds through TikTok LIVE gifts. Any gift mechanic with a chance element or a prize therefore carries real Turkish legal risk on top of TikTok policy risk.

### Cited Findings
- **2020 social media law:** foreign social media platforms must appoint Turkey-based representatives. Non-compliant companies "could face fines, the blocking of advertisements or have bandwidth slashed by up to 90%". YouTube and TikTok complied in 2021. — [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/7228799/turkey-social-media-law); [Euronews, 19 Jan 2021](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2021/01/19/turkey-bans-advertising-on-twitter-and-pinterest-under-social-media-law). The search results describe the mechanisms but do not name Law 7253 directly.
- **2026 Law 7578:** under-15 ban, separated service for ages 15–17, e-Devlet age verification, a representative requirement for online gaming companies, and BTK fines or bandwidth penalties. Details are in Q5. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/23/turkiye-mps-pass-bill-to-restrict-social-media-use-for-children-under-15); [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/bilgi/galeri/15-yas-alti-sosyal-medya-duzenlemesi-resmi-gazete-karari-son-dakika-15-yas-alti-sosyal-medya-yasagi-yururluge-girdi-mi-ne-zaman-43336118)
- **AML/gambling enforcement involving LIVE gifts (Nov 2026, Ankara Chief Public Prosecutor):**
  - In simultaneous operations across 8 provinces, 11 of 14 suspects were caught.
  - The suspects allegedly laundered illegal-betting proceeds by sending virtual gifts to LIVE streamers. The streamers cashed them out and returned the money, minus TikTok's commission, in cash or by bank transfer.
  - MASAK found money movements of 1,928,362,722.98 TRY over six years.
  - Charges are brought under Law 7258 (illegal betting) and laundering of criminal proceeds.
  — [Nefes](https://www.nefes.com.tr/tiktok-fenomenlerine-sanal-hediye-gondererek-para-akladilar-8-ilde-operasyon-163636); [Memurlar.net](https://www.memurlar.net/haber/1183759/tiktok-ta-1-9-milyarlik-kara-para-trafigi.html); [Ehamedya](https://ehamedya.com.tr/haber/tiktokta-sanal-hediye-uzerinden-kara-para-aklama-iddiasi-8-ilde-operasyon)
- **Lotteries and draws:**
  - In Turkey, "the right to plan and organise games of chance with cash prizes and to hold draws is reserved by law to Milli Piyango İdaresi" (translated). Social media draws require a written application to Milli Piyango İdaresi at least 15 days before the draw.
  - Since 2 Feb 2026, permission applications for non-cash lotteries and draws run through MPİ's electronic "Çekilişevi" system.
  - Genuine skill-based contests and prize-free engagement campaigns generally need no permission. However, if a hidden random selection decides the winner, the contest legally counts as a draw.
  - MPİ has opened investigations into influencers running unauthorised giveaways.
  - Reported penalties conflict: 3–5 years' imprisonment plus a judicial fine of up to 10,000 days, versus an administrative fine of up to 10 million TRY and up to 2 years' imprisonment.
  — [Varol Hukuk, Dec 2025](https://www.varolhukuk.com/2025/12/01/sosyal-medya-uzerinden-yapilan-cekilislerde-izin-proseduru-ve-usulsuzluklerin-hukuki-boyutu/); [Dural Hukuk](https://www.duralhukuk.com/blog-detay/sosyal-medya-cekilisleri-yasal-mi-izin-gerektiren-haller); [Barlas Hukuk](https://barlaslaw.com/tr/sosyal-medyada-duzenlenen-cekilisler-ve-hukuki-boyutu/); [TÜRSAB notice](https://www.tursab.org.tr/duyurular/yasal-mevzuat-geregi-cekilis-duzenleme-izni-hakkinda-onemli-bilgilendirme); [Digital Age](https://digitalage.com.tr/fenomenler-icin-baslatilan-korsan-cekilis-sorusturmasinin-hukuki-detaylari/); [Sondakika](https://sondakika.com/haber/haber-sosyal-medya-fenomenlerine-cekilis-sorusturmasi-13862902)
- **Content and culture scrutiny:** Turkish media report "virtual pavyon" (nightclub-style) gift-driven LIVE trends and police detentions over obscene paid LIVE streams. The Ministry of Family and Social Services has sought removal or access blocking and filed criminal complaints in earlier LIVE cases. — [Başka Gazete](https://www.baskagazete.com/haber/tiktok-ta-canli-yayin-furyasi-sanal-pavyon-olusturuluyor-halay-cekiyorlar-259340); [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/tiktokta-para-karsiligi-mustehcen-canli-yayin-yapan-kadin-gozaltina-alindi-42367608); [Habergündemim](https://www.habergundemim.com/tiktokta-canli-yayinlarda-hediye-ekonomisine-operasyon-4441216)

### Inferences
- In Turkey, a gift-triggered game that hands out any prize decided by chance (random winner, spin wheel, loot drop with real-world or transferable value) risks being treated as an unlicensed lottery or draw (MPİ) or a game of chance (7258), in addition to breaching TikTok's gambling rules. Purely cosmetic, deterministic in-game effects with no prize are the defensible design.
- After the Nov 2026 laundering case, high-volume gifting to anonymous or faceless accounts in Turkey may draw extra AML scrutiny from TikTok and MASAK. Transparent identity, an agency affiliation and clean payout records reduce that exposure.

### Gaps
- The text of Law 7253 and Law 7258 (definition of "şans oyunu") was not retrieved from mevzuat.gov.tr.
- No BTK statement was found specifically on TikTok LIVE gifts.
- **Tax:** secondary Turkish blogs mention that social-media income, including gifts, donations and subscriptions, has been addressed since 1 Jan 2022 under provisions added by Law 7338, likely the GVK 20/B content-creator regime. This was not verified against official GİB sources and should be checked separately.
- No Turkish regulator guidance was found that addresses virtual-gift-triggered game mechanics as such.
